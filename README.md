## 🔭 Research Interests

I am primarily interested in **[Mathematical logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_logic)**, with a specific focus on **[Set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Set_theory)**, **[Forcing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forcing_(mathematics))**, and *Forcing with side conditions*.

My further mathematical interests encompass:
* **[Combinatorial set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinitary_combinatorics)** (Infinitary Combinatorics)
* **[Descriptive set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descriptive_set_theory)**
* **[Inner model theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inner_model_theory)** & **[Large cardinals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_cardinal)**
* **[Determinacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Determinacy)** & **[Cardinal invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_characteristics_of_the_continuum)**
